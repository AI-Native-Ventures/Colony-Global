# Company entry implementation and proof gate

The approved O-03 and return-route designs are the product contract. This phase connects a verified account to its companies without loading prototype fixtures. It follows the [repository design](../specs/2026-10-14-colony-global-rebuild-repository-design.md) and [first-run plan](2026-10-14-first-run-auth.md).

The app asks the account relay for self-provisioning configuration and signed `scope=member` membership. No membership opens Scout's business questions; one membership opens that company's signed business profile; several memberships require a choice. Partners can reopen the shared profile. A failed lookup shows a retry state, never a sample company. Company creation is started after the business-name answer and uses a stable, locally saved slug so retry can find a creation whose response was lost. Draft answers are scoped by account, provisioning domain and company and stay on this computer until the owner confirms the summary. At confirmation, the app preserves an existing signed kind:0 person profile or saves the entered name in that company, then writes a signed kind 47046 business-profile action. It advances only after reading both signed records back with matching fields. A failed person-profile write leaves the draft for retry; a member cannot create a missing owner business profile.

The gate is exact-commit GitHub CI: the app lane exercises new-owner creation, returning-owner reopen, draft resume, failed and lost-response person-profile writes, a failed business-profile write and retry, failed discovery, multi-company choice and member permission. The engine lane runs a disposable Postgres/Redis test through the real self-provisioning, NIP-11, kind:0, `/events` and `/query` handlers, including a member write denial. Browser proof uses a controlled native bridge and relay; installed-device, public deployment and real mail are separate gates.

O-03 remains partial after this phase. Google sign-in, starter conversations, team power, hiring and the team-computer decision remain later first-run work. The app's existing Today and other navigation still show explicit unfinished states after the business profile is saved.
