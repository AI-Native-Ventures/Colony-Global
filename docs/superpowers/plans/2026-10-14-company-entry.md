# Company entry implementation and proof gate

The approved O-03 and return-route designs are the product contract. This phase connects a verified account to its companies without loading prototype fixtures. It follows the [repository design](../specs/2026-10-14-colony-global-rebuild-repository-design.md) and [first-run plan](2026-10-14-first-run-auth.md).

The app asks the account relay for self-provisioning configuration and signed `scope=member` membership. No membership opens Scout's business questions; one membership opens that company's signed business profile; several memberships require a choice. A failed lookup shows a retry state, never a sample company. Company creation is started after the business-name answer and uses a stable, locally saved slug so retry can find a creation whose response was lost. Answers stay on this computer until the owner confirms the summary. The first profile write is a signed kind 47046 action, and the app advances only after reading a valid relay-signed kind 30657 head with matching fields. A member cannot create a missing owner profile.

The gate is exact-commit GitHub CI: the app lane exercises new-owner creation, returning-owner reopen, draft resume, a failed profile write and retry, failed discovery, multi-company choice and member permission. The engine lane runs a disposable Postgres/Redis test through the real self-provisioning, NIP-11, `/events` and `/query` handlers, including a member write denial. Browser proof uses a controlled native bridge and relay; installed-device, public deployment and real mail are separate gates.

O-03 remains partial after this phase. The person's answer to “What should I call you?” is retained in the local draft for Scout's setup copy but is not yet written to a per-company person profile. Google sign-in, starter conversations, team power, hiring and the team-computer decision remain later first-run work. The app's existing Today and other navigation still show explicit unfinished states after the business profile is saved.
